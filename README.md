# 📊 Amazon Product Pricing & Review Intelligence Dashboard  
### _A Complete Data Analytics + NLP Sentiment + BI Visualization Project_  

This project analyzes **1,465 Amazon products** across multiple categories to understand how **pricing, discounts & customer feedback affect product performance**.  
Python was used for **EDA + feature engineering + sentiment analysis**, while **Power BI** was used to build an interactive dashboard for business intelligence.

---

## 🚀 Project Goals

✔ Explore how price & discount influence customer engagement  
✔ Identify which product categories perform the best  
✔ Analyze correlations between price & rating  
✔ Apply **NLP sentiment analysis** to customer reviews  
✔ Build a real-time business dashboard showing insights  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1️⃣ Discount vs Popularity
| Discount Range | Avg Popularity |
|---|---:|
| **20–40%** | ⭐ Highest engagement |
| 40–60% | Strong performance |
| 80–100% | Weak perception despite discount |

📌 Moderate discounts work better than extreme discounts.

---

### 2️⃣ Category Performance
📈 *Top-performing categories by popularity:*

- Toys & Games  
- Musical Instruments  
- Electronics  
- Office Products  

---

### 3️⃣ Sentiment Analysis Results (NLP)

| Sentiment | Count | Percentage |
|---|---:|---:|
| 🟢 Positive | 1354 | **92.42%** |
| 🟡 Neutral | 100 | 6.83% |
| 🔴 Negative | 11 | 0.75% |

💡 Customers are overwhelmingly satisfied with Amazon products.
